Where to donate & recycle

Donating functional items that you don't have any use for is a great way to keep our environment clean. When disposing of un-reusable items, there can be certain ways to dispose of things. Down below are some guidelines and restrictions for different items.

Still usable? Donate it

Goodwill
They will accept clothing, shoes, small furniture, electronics, household goods.
Goodwill Locater →
Habitat for Humanity ReStore
They accept furniture, appliances, home decor, and building materials.
Habitat for Humanity Resource →

Broken electronics? Recycle it right

Green E-Waste Recycling Center
ISO-certified e-waste recycler in Milpitas — free drop-off for California residents.
Visit Green E-Waste →
Zero Waste Silicon Valley
Free e-waste drop-off, with free pickup if you have 3 or more items.
Visit Zero Waste Silicon Valley →

Worn-out clothing? Recycle the fabric

H&M Garment Collection
Accepts clothing and textiles in any condition — even ripped or stained — at store locations.
Learn more at H&M →
Uniqlo Garment Recycling
Same as H&M — takes textiles in any condition at store locations.
Learn more at Uniqlo →
